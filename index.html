About Us

Guravayapalem Prabha

Welcome to Guravayapalem Prabha. Guravayapalem Prabha is a devotional group dedicated exclusively to creating beautiful Prabhas in honour of Lord Shiva. Our aim is to express our devotion through artistic and traditional Prabha designs during Shiva festivals and special religious occasions. Every Prabha we create is made with faith, dedication, and respect for our culture and traditions. We believe that our work is a humble offering to Lord Shiva and a way to bring the community together in celebration and devotion. We do not engage in any other activities. Our sole purpose is to design and build Prabhas that inspire devotion and preserve the spiritual heritage of our village.

Our Mission

To create magnificent Prabhas for Lord Shiva with devotion, creativity, and traditional craftsmanship while preserving our cultural and spiritual values.

Vision

To become a symbol of devotion and excellence in Prabha making, inspiring future generations to continue this sacred tradition with dedication and faith.

"Om Namah Shivaya"

Prabha Work Update

Construction and decoration of this year's Prabha have begun with the support of dedicated volunteers and devotees.The preparation and construction work for the grand Guravayapalem Prabha is officially scheduled to commence on February 11, 2027, marking the highly auspicious occasion of Vasant Panchami. Initiating the framework on this sacred day brings immense spiritual blessings as artisans, youth, and village elders come together to begin shaping the massive 100-foot-tall electric structure. While this stands as the fixed timeline for launching the initial craftsmanship, the organizing committee will closely monitor the progress; should there be any adjustments to the schedule or subsequent construction phases, real-time updates will be promptly shared with devotees and followers.
